FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form above or message us on WhatsApp with the type of cleaning, the size of the space and your preferred date. Photos help us give a more accurate price. Quotes are free and there is no obligation.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Vancouver and Metro Vancouver, including Burnaby, Richmond, the North Shore, New Westminster, Coquitlam and Surrey. If your city isn't listed, send us a message.

Can I book a one-time cleaning?

Yes. You can book a one-time clean (like a deep clean or a move-out) or set up recurring visits — weekly, every two weeks or monthly.

Do you clean Airbnb and short-term rentals between guests?

Yes. We handle turnovers between check-out and check-in, following a checklist so every unit is ready for the next guest.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not necessarily. Many clients share access instructions (a key, lockbox or building concierge). We'll agree on the details when you book.
